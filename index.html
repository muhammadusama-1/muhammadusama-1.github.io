A little about me

About Me

I am a Mathematics PhD student at Tulane University. Before joining Tulane, I completed an MS in Mathematics at the Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS) through the International Mathematics Master Fellowship. My previous studies also include an MSc in Mathematics from the University of the Punjab, where I graduated with a perfect CGPA of 4.0/4.0 and received a gold medal.

My master's thesis focused on the Number Field Sieve for integer factorization. This work brought together algebraic number theory, algorithmic ideas, and implementations in Python and SageMath.

The Number Field Sieve

Master's thesis · September 2024 – May 2025

Advisor: Professor Peter Stevenhagen.

Studied the theoretical foundations and major stages of the Number Field Sieve, including polynomial selection, sieving over the integers and number rings, linear algebra, and the final square-root computation. I also implemented the Number Field Sieve and the Quadratic Sieve using Python and SageMath.
