Master's Thesis: The Number Field Sieve

September 2024 – May 2025 · Advisor: Professor Peter Stevenhagen

Studied the theoretical foundations of the Number Field Sieve, including polynomial selection, sieving over the integers and number rings, the collection of smooth relations, the linear algebra phase, and the final square-root computation.

Implemented both the Number Field Sieve and the Quadratic Sieve in Python and SageMath and used them to factor integers.

Number Field Sieve and Quadratic Sieve

Python · SageMath · Integer Factorization

For my master's thesis, I implemented two main algorithms for integer factorization: the Quadratic Sieve (QS) and the Number Field Sieve (NFS). To test my code, I successfully factored the 6th Fermat number using QS, and the number 45,901 using NFS. While these codes are not highly optimized, they serve as useful examples to understand the fundamental principles of these algorithms. They provide insight into how these factorization techniques work in practice, making it easy to follow and understand exactly how both algorithms operate, step by step.
