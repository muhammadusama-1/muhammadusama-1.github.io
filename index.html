About Me

I am a first-year PhD student in the Department of Mathematics at Tulane University. My core academic interests lie within pure mathematics with heavy computational aspects. I am broadly interested in arithmetic geometry and structural properties of algebraic entities.

Research Interests

My research focuses primarily on Elliptic Curves and their properties over finite fields $\mathbb{F}_q$. I am particularly interested in computational methods, counting rational points, and exploring the explicit structure of algebraic structures such as the endomorphism ring $\text{End}(E)$ of a given curve $E/\mathbb{F}_q$.

For example, given an elliptic curve defined by a short Weierstrass equation: $$y^2 = x^3 + ax + b$$ I investigate computational constraints related to the group structure of $E(\mathbb{F}_q)$ and its associated trace of Frobenius $t$, satisfying the Hasse bound $|t| \le 2\sqrt{q}$.
